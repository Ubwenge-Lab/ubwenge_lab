WHY HEALTHCARE

The barriers we're breaking

Healthcare is fundamental to human wellbeing, yet millions across Africa face barriers to accessing quality care. These aren't abstract statistics — they're the daily reality that drives everything we build.

Long wait times

Patients often wait hours — sometimes days — for appointments and consultations, leading to delayed treatment and worsened health outcomes.

Fragmented systems

Hospitals, pharmacies, and clinics operate in silos, creating massive inefficiencies and making care navigation unnecessarily complex.

Medicine access

Finding consistent medication availability requires visiting multiple pharmacies, wasting valuable time and transportation costs for patients.

Information gaps

Patients lack guidance on when they need hospital care versus pharmacy support, leading to unnecessary emergency visits or untreated conditions.
