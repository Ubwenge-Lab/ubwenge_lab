OUR IMPACT & VISION

Transforming healthcare delivery across Africa

We measure success not by downloads or revenue, but by the number of people whose healthcare experience we improve

WHY IT MATTERS

Impact is our north star

At Ubwenge Lab, we measure success not by downloads or revenue, but by the number of people whose healthcare experience we improve. Every feature we build is designed to create measurable, meaningful impact — reaching the patients, pharmacies, and providers who need it most

"Every feature we build is designed to create measurable, meaningful impact on people's lives."

AREAS OF IMPACT

Six dimensions of change

✓

Healthcare Access

We make quality healthcare accessible to everyone. Connecting rural and urban communities to vital services through intelligent digital infrastructure.

⏱

Time Savings

We reduce hours spent searching for care and navigating the system. Saving time boosts productivity and reduces patient stress significantly.

$

Cost Reduction

We help patients avoid unnecessary travel costs. Making healthcare affordable and transparent for everyone, regardless of location or income.

🏥

Hospital Efficiency

We reduce congestion by routing non-urgent cases to pharmacies. This allows hospitals to focus resources on critical patients who truly need them.

📊

Data-Driven Healthcare

We provide actionable insights into trends and service gaps. Innovative data empowers providers to make better-informed, faster decisions.

🌐

Digital Inclusion

Our tech works in low-bandwidth areas and supports multiple languages, ensuring access for users of all literacy levels across Rwanda and beyond.

LONG-TERM STRATEGIC VISION

Our roadmap to continental impact

2026 — FOUNDATION PHASE

Launch & Establish

Launch E-Vuze pharmacy system in Rwanda. 50+ pharmacies onboarded. Initial data gathering and platform refinement based on real-world feedback

2027-2028 — EXPANSION PHASE

Scale Within Rwanda

Roll out hospital and AI triage modules. Expand to additional cities across Rwanda. Reaching 500K+ patients with intelligent, connected healthcare services

2029-2030 — REGIONAL GROWTH

East Africa Expansion

Expand E-Vuze to neighbouring East Africa countries. Launch telemedicine integration and cross-border data sharing for regional healthcare intelligence

2032+ — CONTINENTAL IMPACT

Africa-Wide Platform

Become the leading healthcare infrastructure platform across Africa, serving millions and setting the standard for intelligent, accessible healthcare delivery

IMPACT ON STAKEHOLDERS

Who benefits and how

Our platform creates value across the entire healthcare ecosystem — patients, pharmacies, and providers all experience measurable improvements

For Patients

  • Faster access to needed medications
  • Reduced healthcare costs through price transparency
  • Less time wasted in hospitals for non-urgent issues
  • Better healthcare decisions through AI guidance
  • Improved health outcomes through timely care

For Pharmacies

  • Increased customer reach and visibility
  • More efficient inventory management
  • Reduced time answering stock inquiries
  • Growing customer base through platform
  • Digital transformation support

For Healthcare Providers

  • Reduced congestion in hospitals and clinics
  • Better resource allocation and planning
  • Improved patient satisfaction scores
  • Data insights for service improvement
  • More efficient operations overall
