We Build Platforms, Not Products

At Ubwenge Lab, we don't create one-off applications. We design comprehensive platforms that address systemic healthcare challenges. Each project is built with scalability, sustainability, and real-world impact in mind. Our current focus is on healthcare infrastructure, starting with E-Vuze—a platform that connects the entire healthcare ecosystem.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT

✚ E-Vuze Healthcare Platform

AI-Powered Healthcare One-Stop Center for Rwanda and Beyond

The Problem We're Solving

Healthcare in many African countries is fragmented. Patients struggle to find available medications, hospitals are overwhelmed with non-urgent cases, and there's no intelligent system to guide people toward the right level of care.

Our Solution

E-Vuze creates a unified digital ecosystem where patients find pharmacies instantly, hospitals manage flow efficiently, and AI triage guides care correctly, saving time and improving outcomes.
